Two different splits — don't conflate. The 80 / 20 Bid-Strength weight is opportunity-fit (A) vs funder-fit (B), NOT RFP vs Donor. By requirement source, the RFP⊕Donor denominator is ≈ 48% RFP / 52% Donor (RFP-primary on fit criteria, Donor-primary on the gates) — so we keep 80/20 and don't add a separate RFP:Donor knob (it would double-count).
